**#9 docs/methods.md is out of date** · `docs` `medium` The method text

email links to it, so clients read this text.

**#1 Output not compared with a JUNO run** (spec D12) · `risk` `high`
· closed 2026-10-20: compared; the difference is a DNAcopy bug fix.
`../test20` reran a JUNO project (38 tumors x 20 normals, 760 pairs)
on IRIS beside its JUNO output. Counts, bin grid and `getGeneCalls`
match JUNO exactly; the segmentation does not (1,817 JUNO gene calls,
1,229 IRIS on the same pairs, 1,128 shared, none in the opposite
direction), so the acceptance in spec section 4 step 5 is not met as
run. The cause is DNAcopy 1.75.5, which fixed the weights passed to
each chromosome in weighted segmentation: the IRIS code with DNAcopy
1.60.0 reproduces every JUNO gene call. Decision: stay on the fixed
DNAcopy, do not back port; recorded in `CHANGELOG.md` and
`docs/methods.md`. Report:
`../test20/seqCNA_JUNO_vs_IRIS_concordance.html`.
